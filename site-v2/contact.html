Before you arrive.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 1:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Ask the team if you need a different arrangement.

Yes. We have private AC rooms as well as dorm options. Availability depends on your dates.

Does my stay include Moksha Wellness experiences?

No. Moksha Wellness is a separate partner. Any wellness booking, availability and charges are arranged separately.
